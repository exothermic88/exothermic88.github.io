NCOS 2.0 is here. This is a major release touching nearly every part of the system —
the desktop, the installer, theming, backups, and the ISO itself. Grab it from the
[download page](/#download) and check the [wiki](/wiki/home/) for updated guides.

## NCOS Assistant

- **A local AI assistant, built into the desktop** — the new `ncos-assistant` applet
  sits in your panel and dock. It runs entirely offline on your machine (powered by
  Ollama with a compact chat model and embeddings for retrieval), and it knows the
  NCOS documentation, so you can ask it how to do things on your system without
  sending anything to the cloud. See the [Assistant guide](/wiki/assistant/).

## New in the desktop

- **First-party applets** — a package update applet and a weather applet, both from
  the `[ncos]` repo, ship preconfigured in the panel.
- **COSMIC Store and COSMIC Monitor** are now included for graphical app installs and
  system monitoring. Pamac has been retired in favor of `pacman`, `yay`, and the Store.
- **New default apps** — LocalSend (cross-device file sharing), Popsicle (USB
  flasher), and Android Messages join the lineup.

## Theming and wallpapers

- **Three complete themes** — Nord, Red, and Purple — restyle COSMIC, kitty, and
  VS Code together with one keystroke (`Super+Shift+]` / `Super+Shift+[`).
- **Wallpaper picker** — press `Super+Shift+W` for a visual rofi grid of your
  wallpapers, powered by the new `awww` wallpaper daemon.
- **Multi-monitor panorama wallpapers** — dual-monitor wallpapers are automatically
  split across displays. Full details in the [theming guide](/wiki/theming/).

## Installer and storage

- **btrfs by default** — new installs use btrfs with a full subvolume layout and
  zstd compression.
- **Bootable snapshots** — run `auto-snapshot` once and Timeshift snapshots appear
  right in your GRUB menu via grub-btrfs, so you can boot into a snapshot if an
  update goes wrong. See [Backups & Snapshots](/wiki/backups/).
- **Dual-boot switch** — `multiboot-toggle enable` turns on the multiboot GRUB menu
  and detects other installed operating systems; `disable` returns to the hidden,
  instant boot. See the [dual-boot guide](/wiki/dual-boot/).
- **Rebranded installer** — a redesigned Calamares with new sidebar, navigation, and
  an all-new slideshow.

## Under the hood

- **Slimmer ISO** — trimmed Qt dependencies (over a gigabyte smaller installed) and
  a switch to zstd compression for faster boot and install.
- **Modern graphics stack** — legacy `xf86-video` drivers dropped in favor of
  modesetting.

> Upgrading from 1.0? NCOS is rolling-release — `sudo pacman -Syu` keeps you current.
> The new applets and tools are available from the [NCOS repo](/wiki/ncos-repo/).
